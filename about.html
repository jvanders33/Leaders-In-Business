What we do

Making advisory happen.

Most accounting firms already do advisory. They just do it informally — and everything that makes it valuable is exactly what informal work can't deliver.

Leaders In Business exists to fix that. The Strategic Advisory Operating System gives firms one connected workspace, a proven methodology and an implementation pathway — so advisory becomes structured, paid and repeatable. It's already at work inside networks like DFK and Lumos.

Our founder Brent runs the method in practice — including the monthly Elevate accountability group, where he dials in on the 1st of every month for open mic on where firms are at, what's stuck and what's next.
The promise

We help accounting firms give a lot more good advice.

Not more reports. Not more compliance. More of the advice that changes how a business is run — priced properly, delivered on a method, and shared across the whole team.
