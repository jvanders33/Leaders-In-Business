Buyer questions

The things firms ask us.

QCan I upgrade from Starter?

Yes — upgrade to Activate at any time. Starter is designed for self-starters; Activate adds the coaching, the plan for your own business and the Method training.

QHow many seats do we get?

Starter and Activate include 1–2 seats. Enterprise is licensed for up to 20 users. Extra seats on Starter and Activate are priced per seat — ask us for the current rate.

QWhat's the commitment?

Activate runs for 12 months — that's the arc that takes you from plan to go-to-market and then the full Elevate cycle. Enterprise is a one-off training investment plus a monthly membership.

QDo you work with firms our size?

Solo operators through to mid-tier. Enterprise is what we already do with DFK and Lumos — boutique and mid-tier firms moving the whole team to advisory-led.

QIs this a course or a subscription to a tool?

Neither. It's an operating system — the technology, the skills and the community working together. And it's a membership, not a fixed-term program: it runs for as long as you're licensed.

QWhen can we start?

Onboarding is booked before you can drift — sign up, and the call comes within minutes. Activate cohorts start quarterly; the first cohort is 1 Feb 2027, online, any state.
