The problem

Advisory without overwhelm?

Most firms already do advisory. They just do it informally — and everything that makes it valuable is exactly what informal work can't deliver.

01
Informal

It lives in the partner's head, in an email thread, or in a goodwill gesture. Nothing is documented, nothing is owned.

02
Hard to price

If you can't describe the deliverable, you can't charge for it. Advice gets bundled into compliance and given away.

03
Hard to repeat

Every engagement is a fresh invention. Quality depends entirely on who's in the room and how they're feeling that day.

04
Hard to scale

It can't move beyond the partners. Junior staff have no framework, so capability never leaves the top of the firm.
