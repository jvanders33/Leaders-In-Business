The digital engine

MIKE, your AI co-pilot.

Session prep, Action Plans and progress tracking — drafted for you. You bring the judgement.

MIKE lives inside the workspace and works from your own client data. It prepares the next session before you walk in, drafts the plan while the conversation is fresh, and keeps track of what's moving and what's stuck.

  • Session prep drawn from the client's own history
  • Action Plans drafted, not started from scratch
  • Progress tracking that surfaces what's actually moving
